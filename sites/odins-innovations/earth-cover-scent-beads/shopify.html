How to Use Earth Cover Scent

1. Off-Season Gear Storage

Use year-round. Put 2-3 tablespoons of Earth Cover Scent Beads in a sheer bag in gear storage containers, boot bags, and clothing totes. Continuously infuses equipment with earth scent and prevents musty odors. Ensures gear smells like the forest floor on opening day.

2. Stand-Site Perimeter Defense

Sprinkle beads in a 10-foot radius around tree stands or ground blinds. Creates a scent bubble that maintains coverage. For mobile setups, place 3-5 ounces in a mesh bag and hang in the blind.

3. Access Trail Treatment

Apply Earth Cover Scent Liquid to boot soles, foliage, and trails. Oil-based formula polymerizes for a 7-day weatherproof layer per application.
