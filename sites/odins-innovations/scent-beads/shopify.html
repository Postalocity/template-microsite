Seasonal Guide

When to Use Odin's Synthetic Scent Beads

Synthetic scent beads provide consistent attraction across all phases of the hunting season. Deploy them early to establish patterns, use them during peak activity for focused results, and maintain them through the late season for consistent performance.

Whitetail Rut Calendar

Pre-SeasonAug - Sept

Establish scent patterns early to condition deer to visit your location. Use food-based scents like Apple or Acorn for curiosity and conditioning.

Pre-RutOct 15 - Oct 31

Bucks begin establishing territory. Use sparingly to create curiosity without spooking deer. Scrape blends work well during this transition.

Peak RutNov 1 - Nov 15

Maximum activity period. Doe Estrus and Dominant Buck scents are most effective. Aggressive deployment at scrape lines and trail junctions.

Post-RutNov 16 - Dec

Late-season opportunities with food-based and curiosity scents. Deer are weary but still responsive to reliable food sources.

How to Use Odin's Scent Beads

  1. 1

    Apply Scent Beads

    Place a small amount of beads in mock scrapes, on drag lines, or around stands. The biodegradable polymer matrix releases lab-formulated attractants steadily for 30+ days.

  2. 2

    Add Liquid Trail

    For drag lines and scent wicks, apply 3-5 drops of liquid scent every 20-30 yards. The concentrated formula provides a strong scent trail that lasts.

Seal unused beads in a Ziplock bag to preserve potency. Store in a cool, dry place for indefinite shelf life.
