How Mosquitoes Locate Hosts — Three-Stage Targeting Process

Understanding the three-stage targeting process

STAGE 1

CO₂ Detection

Mosquitoes detect carbon dioxide from breath at distances over 150 feet. This long-range cue triggers host-seeking behavior.

STAGE 2

Skin Chemistry

Lactic acid and octenol from skin and sweat provide medium-range targeting. Output increases during physical activity like hiking to setups.

STAGE 3

Body Heat

At close range, body heat and moisture guide mosquitoes to landing sites. Citronella's vapor release creates a masking perimeter.
