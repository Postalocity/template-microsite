When to Deploy Doe Estrus

Maximize your success by timing your scent deployment with the whitetail rut phases. Each phase requires a different strategy for optimal results.

Whitetail Rut Calendar

Pre-RutOct 1 - Oct 20

Use scrape blends to condition areas. Save pure doe estrus for later when bucks are actively seeking.

Seeking PhaseOct 21 - Nov 10

Deploy in travel corridors and scrape lines. Bucks begin cruising for receptive does.

Peak RutNov 11 - Nov 25

Prime time! Apply 3 oz per 4'×4' zone. Pair with food-scent beads to anchor deer in the area.

Late RutNov 26 - Dec 15

Secondary rut activity. Focus on food sources. Last chance for mature buck movement.

How to Use

  1. 1

    Sprinkle approximately 3 oz of scent beads evenly across a 4' x 4' area

  2. 2

    Place in natural travel lanes, existing scrapes, or mock scrapes

  3. 3

    Position where visible to trail cameras for documentation with minimal disturbance

  4. 4

    Apply liquid formula to create directional trails leading to bead deployments

  5. 5

    Use on trails, brush, or boot soles for broader coverage

  6. 6

    Reapply liquid every 7–10 days; beads last weeks regardless of weather

Combining both bead and liquid formats provides immediate and sustained performance.
