How to Build an Effective Mock Scrape

  1. 1

    Select a location along a natural travel corridor, field edge, or transition zone between bedding and feeding areas. Look for an overhanging limb (licking branch) 4–5 feet off the ground.

  2. 2

    Clear a 3' x 3' area of leaves and debris down to bare soil beneath the licking branch.

  3. 3

    Sprinkle 1–2 oz of Odin's Scrape Blend scent beads directly in the cleared area.

  4. 4

    Apply Scrape Blend liquid to the licking branch itself — squirt it directly on the branch where a buck would make contact.

  5. 5

    Set your trail camera 10–15 feet from the scrape, angled to capture the full scrape and licking branch.

The scent beads will work continuously for 30+ days regardless of weather. Unlike natural urine applied to a scrape (which washes away with the first rain), Odin's polymer beads are rain proof and continue releasing scent through rain, snow, and heavy dew.

Seasonal Guide

When to Create Mock Scrapes

Pre-RutEarly–Mid October

A mock scrape for whitetail should be set up using Odin's Scrape Blend, using the beads and the liquid. Bucks are establishing territory and beginning to mark scrape lines. 30-day beads start working immediately and last through the rut. Ideal time to pair with trail cameras.

Seeking PhaseLate Oct – Early Nov

Scrape activity peaks as bucks intensify territorial behavior and begin seeking does. Refresh with liquid on subsequent visits. Transition from Scrape Blend to Doe Estrus as local does begin entering estrus.

Peak RutMid November

Natural scrape activity may decline as bucks shift to active doe-chasing. Transition fully to doe estrus scent products.

Why the Right Scent Matters

Synthetic vs Natural Scrape Scent

Traditional mock scrape tactics involve pouring natural buck urine or tarsal gland scent into the scrape. This works for 24–48 hours until the scent degrades, soaks into the soil, or gets washed away by weather. Early in the season, scrapes can go dormant if there is not enough activity for visiting bucks to keep it alive. Odin's 30-day life ensures scents to attract bucks will be present when they start rolling by. Odin's Scrape Blend beads release continuously and consistently for 30+ days, meaning your mock scrape is actively producing scent even when you're not there. You don't need to go check on it, refresh it or replace it. Also, fewer field visits mean less human scent contamination at your setup — the most common reason mock scrapes fail. The National Deer Association offers some helpful information. Learn more from National Deer Association's: "A Guide to Rubs and Scrapes, The Social Media of the Deer World".
