Scent Beads

Non-toxic synthetic scents, releases scents consistently for 30-days and then the bead biodegrades. BETTER THAN YOU FOUND IT ™

Liquid Scents

1

Oil-based formulations that adhere to any surface — branches, bushes, tree trunks

2

Each application releases scent for approximately 7 days

3

Complement beads — use for scent trails, licking branches, trees, adheres to foliage/bushes or squirt it on the bottom of your boots and on e-bike tires.

4

Application: Squirt directly on trees, brush, licking branches, boot soles, or the ground. Natural oil blended with synthetic scents; the oil-based delivery adheres as the scent dissipates and lifts into the air.

5

Squeeze bottle provides controlled, targeted application

6

Size: 4 oz bottle ($14.95)

7

Refresh every 7 days or after heavy rain

8

Pro tip: Coat e-bike tires or boot soles with liquid scent to create a scent trail leading to your bead deployment site. Multi-year shelf life; will not spoil and scent remains when sealed.

Pro Tips

Advanced Tips

Layering Multiple Scents

Doe Estrus beads + Earth Cover Scent beads around the stand. Dominant Buck on one side + Doe Estrus on the other to simulate a buck tending a doe.

Wind and Scent Placement

Deploy attractant beads downwind of expected deer approach. Place cover scent beads upwind of stand position.

Scent Refresh Protocol

Beads — refresh every 30 days or when scent output diminishes. Do not remove old beads — they biodegrade naturally. Liquid — refresh every 7 days or after heavy rain.
