How to Use Odin's Food Scent Attractants

  1. 1

    Sprinkle approximately 3 oz of scent beads evenly across a 4' × 4' area at your target location.

  2. 2

    Place beads in front of trail cameras for 30+ days of continuous monitoring.

  3. 3

    Use liquid to create scent trails leading to the bead deployment — squirt on trees, brush, and the ground along natural travel routes.

  4. 4

    For bear and feral pig hunting: apply liquid generously and let the animals walk through the oil-based scent. They will track it in every direction they go, creating a natural scent distribution network.

The biodegradable polymer beads release scent continuously for 30+ days regardless of weather. Rain and snow don't wash away the scent — they protect it until air exposes it.

Baiting Laws &
Scent Attractants

Know the difference. Stay legal in every state.

Scent Attractants

  • ✓No consumable food — deer investigate repeatedly but find nothing to eat. The aroma is too strong to resist.
  • ✓Zero nutritional value — not classified as feed or bait in most states
  • ✓No salt, minerals or nutritional value — eliminates the three most common baiting triggers

Traditional Bait (Corn, Feed)

  • ✗Consumable food — deer eat it, providing nutritional value
  • ✗Classified as bait in most states — restricted or illegal in many areas
  • ✗Illegal entirely in many states or limited use in controlled areas

Important: Regulations vary by state. Always check your state's specific baiting and attractant regulations before deployment. Odin's scent attractants are legal in most jurisdictions, but local laws may differ.
