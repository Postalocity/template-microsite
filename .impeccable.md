## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary Audience**: Deer hunters, primarily male, ages 25-65
- **Context**: Hunters planning their scent strategy for the season, researching products while at home or in the field
- **Job to be done**: Find reliable, long-lasting scent attractants that outperform natural urine and are legal in their state
- **Key concerns**: Effectiveness, legality (CWD restrictions), weather resistance, ease of use, value for money
- **Emotional goals**: Confidence in product performance, trust in brand, reassurance about legal compliance, excitement for hunting success

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Professional, knowledgeable, scientific yet accessible
- **Tone**: Trustworthy, performance-driven, confident but not arrogant
- **3-word personality**: Professional, reliable, innovative
- **Emotional goals**: Trust, confidence, performance assurance

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Clean, bold, product-focused, outdoorsy without being cliché
- **Style reference**: Sitka Gear, Under Armour Hunting, Cabela's - professional outdoor brands
- **Anti-references**: Avoid cheap/tacky hunting imagery, neon colors, overly rustic/woodsy clichés
- **Theme**: Professional outdoor brand - think "premium hunting gear" not "bargain bin"

### Design Principles
1. **Trust through transparency** - Lab data, certifications, and clear comparisons build credibility
2. **Performance over promises** - Let the science and specs speak, avoid hyperbole
3. **Clarity in complexity** - Make technical information (polymer technology, CWD safety) accessible
4. **Mobile-first field use** - Design for hunters checking products on their phone in the field
5. **Conversion through education** - Long-form content that informs and converts simultaneously

### Brand Colors
- **Primary**: Deep forest green (h:140, s:60, l:25) - trustworthy, outdoorsy
- **Secondary**: Burnt orange (h:25, s:85, l:45) - hunting tradition, action, CTA emphasis
- **Accent**: Warm gold (h:45, s:90, l:55) - premium quality, "best seller" badges
- **Background**: Off-white/cream (h:40, s:5, l:97) - easier on eyes than pure white
- **Text**: Charcoal (h:220, s:15, l:15) - softer than pure black

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Agency FB (bold, condensed, outdoor feel, uppercase)
- **Body**: Source Sans 3 or Open Sans (readable for long articles)
- **Scale**: Modular type scale with fluid sizing using clamp()

### Key Differentiators
- Made in USA
- 30+ days continuous release (52% after 27 days lab tested)
- CWD-safe and legal in all 50 states
- Biodegradable polymer
- Weatherproof (rain/snow/dew resistant)

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- Generic AI aesthetics (purple gradients, glassmorphism, rounded everything)
- Center-aligned text walls
- Card overload (everything in identical boxes)
- Missing trust signals
- Insufficient CTAs
- Poor mobile experience
- Relying on color alone to convey meaning (accessibility)
